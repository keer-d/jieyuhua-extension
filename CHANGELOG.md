# Product Changelog

FluentLoop grew from the earlier Jieyuhua inline text explainer through frequent personal-use iterations. This file keeps the major product shifts; minor internal builds are intentionally omitted.

## Current — 2.23

- FluentLoop identity across the extension and learning surfaces
- English and French learning spaces
- Inline, Side Panel, and Full Studio workflow over one local store
- Article, conversation, selection, and subtitle capture
- SM-2 review, mixed practice, knowledge realms, and notes

## 2.7 — Subtitle capture

- YouTube and Bilibili subtitle hover explanations
- Short local cache for repeated lookups
- Search words flow into the learning shelf
- Dark, warm-gold product interface

## 2.6 — Context-aware explanations

- Shift-hover lookup on ordinary webpages
- Topic-aware explanations instead of a finance-only prompt
- Recent-question memory to reduce repeated quiz items

## 2.5 — French learning support

- Unicode-safe pronunciation chips for accented words
- Language-specific voices and prompts
- French grammar and conjugation-aware study material

## 2.4 — Quiz validity

- Deterministic validation that multiple-choice answers exist in the options
- Accurate translation before explanation
- Shared quiz logic across Side Panel and Full Studio

## 2.3 — Language-separated learning stores

- English/French switch with independent shelves and review queues
- Translation, sentence-writing, and conjugation question types
- Direct article capture from the Side Panel

## 2.2 — Capture and shelf improvements

- More reliable inline bubble positioning
- Hard-word selection instead of indiscriminate vocabulary capture
- Conversations can be condensed into reusable learning cards

## 2.0 — From explainer to learning loop

- Article shelf and tag-derived knowledge realms
- Full-tab deep-reading Studio
- Exams and scheduled review
- Shared local learning state across extension surfaces

## 1.x — Jieyuhua

- Inline explanation for selected webpage text
- Streaming multi-provider responses
- Side Panel chat and local model settings
